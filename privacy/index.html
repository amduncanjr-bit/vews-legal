Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 15, 2025

VEWS ("we," "our," or "us") operates the VEWS mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you access or use our Service. By using VEWS, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the VEWS application, including content creators, viewers, and any other individuals who access our Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register for an account, create content, or interact with our Service, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, password, date of birth, and profile photo
  • Profile Information: Biography, social media links, location, and other information you choose to share
  • Content: Videos, images, comments, messages, and other content you create, upload, or share
  • Communications: Information you provide when you contact our support team or respond to surveys
  • Payment Information: If you make purchases or receive payments, we collect billing details, payment card information (processed by secure third-party payment processors), and transaction history
  • Identity Verification: Government-issued ID, selfie photos, and other verification documents when required for creator verification

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Service, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers (IDFA, GAID), hardware model, and mobile network information
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, access times, pages viewed, app crashes, and other system activity
  • Location Data: General location based on IP address; precise location only with your explicit consent
  • Usage Data: Features used, content viewed, search queries, interactions with other users, and time spent on the Service
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies, pixels, and local storage to collect information about your browsing behavior and preferences

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Social Media Platforms: If you link your account or sign in using third-party services (e.g., Google, Apple, Facebook), we receive profile information authorized by those platforms
  • Analytics Partners: We receive aggregated analytics data from third-party services
  • Advertising Partners: Information about your interactions with advertisements
  • Other Users: Information that other users provide about you, such as when they tag you in content or send you messages

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and Maintain the Service: Create and manage your account, enable you to create and share content, and facilitate interactions between users
  • Personalize Your Experience: Customize content recommendations, suggest accounts to follow, and tailor your feed based on your interests and activity
  • Process Transactions: Handle payments, subscriptions, tips, and payouts to creators
  • Communicate with You: Send service-related announcements, respond to inquiries, and provide customer support
  • Send Marketing Communications: With your consent, send promotional messages about new features, offers, and events
  • Improve Our Service: Analyze usage patterns, conduct research, and develop new features
  • Ensure Safety and Security: Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, spam, and security threats
  • Enforce Policies: Enforce our Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and other policies
  • Comply with Legal Obligations: Respond to legal requests, protect our rights, and comply with applicable laws
  • Advertising: Display personalized advertisements and measure ad effectiveness

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

3.1 With Other Users

  • Your profile information and public content are visible to other users
  • When you interact with other users (comments, messages, follows), those interactions are visible to relevant parties
  • Content you share may be reshared by other users within or outside the platform

3.2 With Service Providers

We share information with third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf, including:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers
  • Payment processors and financial institutions
  • Analytics and performance monitoring services
  • Customer support and communication platforms
  • Content moderation and safety services
  • Identity verification providers

3.3 With Advertising Partners

We share information with advertising partners to deliver personalized ads and measure their effectiveness. This may include device identifiers, demographic information, and interests.

3.4 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information when required by law or in response to:

  • Court orders, subpoenas, or legal processes
  • Government or law enforcement requests
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of VEWS, our users, or the public
  • To detect, prevent, or address fraud, security, or technical issues

3.5 Business Transfers

If VEWS is involved in a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

3.6 With Your Consent

We may share your information in other circumstances with your explicit consent.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide you with the Service and maintain your account
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax, accounting, legal hold requirements)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements
  • Protect against fraud and abuse

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes or where deletion is not technically feasible.

Content you have shared may continue to appear on the Service if it has been reshared by others or if deletion is not technically feasible.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • Access controls and authentication requirements
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Incident response procedures

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You can request a copy of your personal information in a structured, commonly used format.

6.2 Correction

You can update or correct inaccurate information through your account settings or by contacting us.

You can request deletion of your account and personal information. Some information may be retained as described in Section 4.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by:

6.7 Withdraw Consent

Where we rely on consent to process your information, you can withdraw consent at any time through your settings.

  • Right to Non-Discrimination: You will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@vewsapp.com. We will verify your identity before processing your request.
