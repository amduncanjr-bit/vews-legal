Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Vews has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everything on Vews: live streams, recordings and clips, posts, photos and videos, profiles, usernames, comments, direct and group messages, and subscriber content.

What is not allowed

  • Child sexual abuse material of any kind, real or generated.
  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18.
  • Grooming, or contacting a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, or threatening to share intimate images of a minor.
  • Trafficking or the sexual exploitation of children.
  • Content that sexualizes minors, including comments, links and requests.

How to report

Anyone can report a problem, in the app or by email:

  • In the app, on the content: use Report on any post, comment, live, live chat message, profile or message thread, and pick Child safety. Reports go to the Vews team for review, and child safety reports alert the child safety contact right away.
  • In the app, from Settings: go to Settings > Report a problem, choose A child safety concern, describe what you saw and tap Send. You never have to leave the app.
  • By email: write to josh@vewsapp.com with the username, a link if you have one, and what you saw. Do not attach or forward illegal images.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first.

What we do

  • We review reports of child safety violations as a priority.
  • We remove violating content and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law enforcement, as the law requires.
  • We keep the records the law requires and cooperate with law enforcement investigations.
  • Members can block other accounts, which stops them from messaging each other.

Compliance

Vews complies with child safety laws in the places where it operates, including the reporting duties of online service providers in the United States.

Child safety contact

Vews has a designated point of contact for child safety questions from users, parents, regulators and app stores: josh@vewsapp.com. This is also the child safety contact on file with Google Play for the Vews app.

See also our Terms of Use, section 17, "Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Exploitation".
